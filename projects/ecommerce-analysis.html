E-Commerce Sales Analysis

An analysis of 96,478 delivered orders from Olist, a Brazilian online marketplace: how revenue grew, what sold, where customers are, and an RFM segmentation of all 93,358 customers with an action for each segment.

The question

What actually drives revenue on the marketplace, and who are the customers? The answer had to hold up: a first version of the analysis called May to August the best season, but the data covers January–August for two years and September–December for only one, which made those months look stronger. Comparing each month with the same month a year earlier showed the real story: growth, not seasonality.

Approach

  1. Clean and merge. Orders, customers, items, payments and products (110,197 item rows); kept the 96,478 delivered orders, summed multiple payments per order and translated categories to English.
  2. Explore. Revenue trend, year-over-year comparison, categories, states, order values and order timing.
  3. Segment with RFM. Scored every customer on recency, frequency and monetary value and assigned 8 rule-based segments, each with a suggested business action.
  4. Make it reproducible. One command downloads the data; GitHub Actions re-runs every notebook on each push and fails if the results change.

Retention is the main problem: 97% of customers never came back. Converting even a small share of the 36,224 recent first-time buyers into repeat customers would matter more than anything else in the data.

Limitations

  • Less than two full years of data, so seasonality beyond Black Friday cannot be measured reliably.
  • RFM thresholds are relative to this dataset, and the segment rules are a common convention rather than fitted to outcomes.
